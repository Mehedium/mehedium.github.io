• Tap a numbered box to tick a set, or the big box beside the name to tick the whole exercise.
  • Tap a weight to change it. It is saved on this phone and shared by the same exercise on other days.
  • Finish Day saves today’s date, clears the ticks and moves you to the next day. The app reopens on the day you were last on.
  • A dark-blue outline around two exercises marks a superset: one set of each, then rest. “new” means I added it. DUP means it is similar to a core exercise.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes between heavy sets. Add weight once you hit the top of the rep range on every set.
